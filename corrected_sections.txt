Scope and Limitations

The scope of the system includes several key processes: verifying user credentials and role-based access to ensure secure access per user responsibility; encoding inventory records for assets and resources to maintain comprehensive information; updating asset quantities, conditions, and accountability records; generating QR-based property stickers for identification; printing official reports such as the Report on the Physical Count of Property, Plant and Equipment (Appendix 73), Inventory Custodian Slip (ICS), Property Acknowledgement Receipt (PAR), and Inventory Transfer Report (ITR); recording audit trails and system activity logs; managing user roles and office assignments; and processing requisition and procurement requests through a centralized workflow. The system also provides specialized dashboards tailored to each user role, including the Administrator, Office Head, and Accounting staff, to ensure that each user views only the information and functions relevant to their responsibilities. Additionally, the system supports offline data entry, where edits made without internet connectivity are queued locally and automatically synchronized once connectivity is restored.

The limitation of the system is that it relies on internet connectivity for full real-time synchronization and cloud-based operations; however, the system includes an offline mode that queues edits locally and syncs them upon reconnection. It will not be integrated with external government databases, accounting systems, or other Local Government Unit platforms. Instead, the system will be specifically tailored for use by LGU Tibiao. Additionally, the processes implemented within this system may differ from those used by other municipalities, making it unique to LGU Tibiao's needs and operational requirements.
