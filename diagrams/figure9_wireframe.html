Figure 9. Complete wireframe of the Centralized Inventory and Resource Tracking System of LGU Tibiao, showing all screens for each user role — Administrator (full system access: dashboard, user management, office management, inventory, QR stickers, requisitions, audit trail, database), Office Head (office dashboard, view assets, submit requisitions, requisition history), and Accountant (financial dashboard, financial review of requisitions, procurement tracking), along with shared screens accessible to all roles (login, notifications, profile, reports, offline mode).
